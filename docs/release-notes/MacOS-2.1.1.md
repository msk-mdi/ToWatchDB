## What's new
- Top Genres in Stats now shows each genre's name next to its bar instead of on top of it.
- Opening a list no longer pauses the app while it works out every show's progress.
- A new Mac's first Dropbox sync, or importing a backup, no longer freezes the window.
- Launching no longer looks for duplicate titles before the window appears.
- Show pages load faster: the app checks which episodes have notes once per page instead of once per row.
- When your Seerr server can't be reached, title pages stop waiting up to 15 seconds for it. They try again after 5 minutes, or right away after Test Connection.
- Posters you scroll past are no longer downloaded. In Low Data Mode, posters are smaller and backdrops aren't downloaded.
- Titles the launch refresh couldn't reach are refreshed again the next time you come back to the app.
