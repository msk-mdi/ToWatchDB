## What's new
- Asking Siri for your next episodes or your watch stats no longer freezes the app while it goes through your library.
- Smart lists, poster menus, the Shortcuts show picker, and title pages do much less work after each change.
- Show pages, including ones open in other windows or kept in the background, no longer redraw on every change elsewhere in your library.
- Dropbox sync no longer goes through your whole library after a background refresh when there's nothing to upload, and each sync does less work.
- Stats loads faster: it reads your library once for the period and the list of years.
- Refreshing a show reads TMDB's answer once, and the refresh skips shows that have ended.
- Seerr isn't asked about a title again each time you switch back to its page.
- Fixed: a poster could turn blank and stay blank after the app freed memory.
